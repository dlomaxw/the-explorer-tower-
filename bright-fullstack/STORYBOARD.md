## Video direction

- **One world**: near-black ground (#0A0A0A) with a faint yellow radial; Montserrat for everything the viewer reads, IBM Plex Mono only for the terminal and endpoint text (to confirm); yellow #FAE104 is the ONLY accent.
- **Object-bridge**: the yellow caret `_` of the page hero. It types, falls, lands as a page edge, becomes the pipeline, the ring, the question mark, then the end-card caret.
- **Components**: terminal, browser and phone mockups in 3D, icon tiles that draw on, charts and numbers that roll, pipeline, sprint ring, shield, 48 h ring, contact card, one CTA with a cursor click.
- **Invisible seams**: every frame hands over to the next (handoff_out copied word for word into handoff_in), no hard cut; the last frame cuts to the ground colour.
- **Text**: the voice sentence as a subtitle in the band y 1450 to 1640 (word by word, 0-2 frames early, ONE key-word box per sentence); display moments (frame 1 title, « Ready to ship? », the number) replace the subtitle.
- **Facts only from the page**: services, the four promises, the 48 h offer, address, phone, email, industries, technologies; « CRM · ERP » is user-stated. No prices, no clients, no statistics beyond the page's own board numbers (24, 12, 8, 6, shown as the page shows them).
- **Music**: scene changes and icon draws land on the beat grid (0.571 s) when the voice cue allows it (±0.05 s).
- **Safe area**: 60 px side margins, bottom 280 px free of anything but the subtitle band.

- Mécanisme 1 « Le caret »: 0.00 (blink), 2.40, 2.93, 12.00, 25.60 (turns into ?), 28.0 (loses blink), 40.7 (end hold)
