## Concepts (one line each)

1. **The Build** (recommended): the page's own terminal opens the film (`npm run build`, « Ready. Your product is live. »), the
   build output becomes the six layers, then the CRM, the four promises, the 48-hour offer.
2. **Every Layer**: a 3D stack of six slabs (web, mobile, backend, CRM, software, DevOps) that the camera descends.
3. **Your CRM, Your Way**: opens on the Bright CRM dashboard (24 / 12 / 8 / 6) and the ten industries, then the rest of the services.
