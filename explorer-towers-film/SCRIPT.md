# Explorer Towers — launch film script (step 1, APPROVED: version A)

Source of every fact below: `site/src/content/site.ts` (approved entries only). Nothing about completion date,
penthouse price, or floor areas is claimed: the site marks those as not yet released.

Format proposed: 16:9, about 45 s, English voice-over, ~125 words.

## Concepts (one line each)

1. **The Curve** — one balcony line wraps the tower; the camera follows it from the street to the roof, then lands on contact details.
2. **Above Kampala** — a night-to-dusk rise from Acacia Avenue to the penthouse pool, the city lights as the closing shot.
3. **Find Us** — opens on the map pin (Kololo), zooms down to the tower, then the offer, then how to reach us.
4. **Three Homes** — two-bed, three-bed, penthouse shown as three doors, each with its price line (or "on application").
5. **A Day in Kololo** — morning gym and garden, afternoon lounge, evening pool: the amenities as a day, ending on "come and visit".
6. **The Number** — opens on "USD 300,000", then reveals what that buys, ending on the phone number.
