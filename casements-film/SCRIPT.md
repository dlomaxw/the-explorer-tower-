## Concepts (one line each)

1. **Twelve in one** — a single window frame grows into each of the twelve product lines, one every 2 s, then pulls back to the whole factory.
2. **Blueprint to building** — a line drawing becomes a window, a wall, a tower; the four steps of your process as the thread.
3. **Four materials** — aluminium, glass, steel, wood: each material owns three product lines and one colour accent.
4. **Kampala skyline** — the real projects (Crested Towers, Petroleum House, Speke Resort) with the products that made them.
5. **Genuine, not counterfeit** — opens on the site's own claim that counterfeit aluminium is common in Uganda; every profile Casements uses is certified genuine.
6. **One factory** — the Industrial Area workshop as the world; the camera walks the shop floor past every product line.
